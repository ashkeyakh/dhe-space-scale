# Attribution and data

Project author: Amin Gholami. Repository: https://github.com/ashkeyakh/dhe-space-scale.

The DHE pointer/navigation convention follows Boguslawski & Gold (2016),
"The Dual Half-Edge," https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi5020019.
This project implements its own batch construction and transition-boundary surgery.

The portable footprints in `demo_data.py` are synthetic examples created for this
project. The original shapefile and its sidecars are not distributed. Example
screenshots illustrate the research workbench; they are not a replacement dataset.
